# Melanoma with Contralateral Axillary SLN Drainage — Case Report Hub

> [!abstract] One-line summary
> Recurrent cutaneous melanoma, left side, in a patient with a **left** axillary SLNB ~20 years prior. Preoperative Tc-99m lymphoscintigraphy localised the sentinel node to the **right (contralateral) axilla**; intraoperative ICG fluorescence traced the aberrant lymphatic channel in real time, captured in full HD — a rare strength, since most published lymphatic-mapping figures in this vault's literature are low-resolution nuclear medicine images, not clear optical footage of the channel itself. Standard dual-tracer practice at QVH — not a trial case. The case argues that ICG resolves anatomical detail (the channel's course) that scintigraphy's activity map alone cannot, in a rare aberrant-drainage pattern with an unusually long (~20-year) latency from the original surgery.
