# Red-Capping ICG SLNB Comparative Analysis

🔗 https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/08998280.2026.2676916

70 patients: 47 ICG alone, 23 ICG + red-capping technique.

## Key finding
Both imaging modes achieved a **100% detection rate** when verified by radiotracer.

## My take
Another data point for "ICG performs at parity with dual-tracer confirmation" — useful as a supporting citation alongside Vyas et al. and Duarte-Bateman et al.
