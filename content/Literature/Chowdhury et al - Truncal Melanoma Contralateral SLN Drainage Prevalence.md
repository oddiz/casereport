# Chowdhury et al — Truncal Melanoma Contralateral SLN Drainage Prevalence

![[1-s2.0-S1748681525001317.pdf]]

Retrospective review, The Christie NHS Foundation Trust, Manchester, UK, Oct 2006–Nov 2024.

> [!failure] Correction — see [[Fact-Check Log]]
> Original note said "1300 patients... 1.1%... most relevant location was at the back, 21%." Corrected below.

## Verified findings
- **1308 consecutive patients** (not 1300) underwent WLE + SLNB for truncal melanoma
- Contralateral drainage occurred in **14 patients (1.1%)**
- Location of primary scar in the 14: left lower back 4 (28.6%), right upper back 4 (28.6%), right lower back 2 (14.3%), right upper abdomen 2 (14.3%), left lower abdomen 1 (7.1%), right upper chest 1 (7.1%) → **10/14 (71%) posterior torso**, of which 6/14 (43%) specifically lower back
- **3 patients (21.4%) experienced melanoma recurrence** during follow-up — this is the recurrence rate, *not* a location statistic
- Authors hypothesise congenital lymphatic variation as a contributing factor

## My take
This is our closest epidemiological comparator — same drainage direction question (truncal melanoma → contralateral axilla) as our case. Use the corrected numbers above directly in the Introduction/Discussion.
